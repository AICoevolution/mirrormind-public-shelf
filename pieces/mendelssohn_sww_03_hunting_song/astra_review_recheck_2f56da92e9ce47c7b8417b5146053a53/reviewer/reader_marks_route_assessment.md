# Reader MARKS/ROUTE assessment before candidate access

All 102 regions in each of three readers were extracted and compared with the Phase 1 print inventory. The extract is source evidence, not approved notation. Fingering numerals are excluded from the intended delivery.

SmartScore loses most staccatissimo wedges (often substitutes staccato), many sf/ff distinctions, tempo spelling, cresc./dimin. text, and pedal signs. It imports running titles/copyright/folio text as directions. It has no adequate P026–P027 ottava and fragments the continuous P088–P100 octave span. P102 fermatas are absent. Its false tuplets at P023, P063, P064, P085 and P092 have no printed tuplet numerals; preserve ordinary written note values. P090 dotted-sixteenth timing is also unsupported.

PlayScore retains more triangular articulations and both P102 fermatas but omits the tempo and essentially all pedal/expressive text. Several sf/ff become f (P024, P051–P052, P063), and accents are missing or become tenuto. It invents a tremolo ornament at P065. P013–P014 and P090 tuplets are not printed. Some octave stops have no valid matching printed start; the continuous P088–P100 span must be reconstructed from print, not copied as separate system-level playback shifts.

Audiveris often converts wedges into accents/dots or words, splits ff into separate f directions, and invents p/mp/pp, breath marks and ornaments. The inverted mordents at P085/P094 are not printed. P019/P063/P076/P086 tuplets are spurious. Its P087 lower octave shift has no printed basis. Most pedal text is missing. P102 fermatas are absent.

All readers retain forward repeat before ordinal6 and backward repeat after ordinal30, and final barline after ordinal102. SmartScore/Audiveris retain the double bar after P005. No printed alternate ending, segno, coda, DC/DS or additional navigation is supported. The half-bars remain physical regions; do not expand or renumber during source comparison.

Slur/tie counts are not endpoint certification. Every reader has omissions, extra spans or misattachment. The full print inventory and per-system pixels remain the independent basis, especially P013–P018 and P059–P062 independent voices, P069–P077 held melody, and P088–P095 lower-staff member-level curves. P010 opening dot and P092–P094 overlapping curves remain local interpretation questions. No reader is accepted as a complete MARKS/ROUTE source.
